Trolls vs Vikings Fjord Fury - Game project (2025)





Overview:

Release: 2025

Studio size: 23

Development: 180hours/30 Days

Role: 3D artist

Tools: Blender, GIMP



About:

This was a game i worked on as part of a university course in game design at Kristiania. The course was aimed at giving us the experience of working in a larger studio setting. We were 23 students on the project. My role was that of a 3D artist.



I was in charge of creating several of the hero assets. My first task was to create the player ship and art bible technical guidelines.

Later i worked with one of the level designers to implement a feature assett of their level; a statue with a sword that would fall down as the player sailed past.



During the final weeks of the project i was doing the art pass on the valhalla level; which included creating a new skybox, sculpting terrain and modeling a few background assets.
